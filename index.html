About

I’m Uwe Dauernheim, a software engineer, engineering leader, and lifelong builder based in Stockholm.

Over the years, I’ve moved from backend engineering and software architecture into leading technical teams and organizations at companies including Voi, Grace Health, Northvolt, and Aris Machina. My work has taken me through urban mobility, women’s health, sustainable energy, advanced manufacturing, data, and AI.

What connects those experiences is a desire to make difficult things useful: turning technical complexity into dependable products, helping talented people do their best work, and building organizations that can grow without losing clarity or humanity.

What I’m exploring now

I’m taking time to train, travel, research emerging developments in AI, and think carefully about the next chapter of my career.

I’m particularly interested in engineering leadership where software and AI meet consequential, real world problems: AI native products, physical systems, advanced industry, health, sustainability, or sport.

How I work

Close to the technology

I stay close enough to the technology to understand the real challenges, while giving people the context and autonomy they need to solve them well.

Good questions over every answer

My role is to ask good questions, create clarity, share knowledge, and help teams make thoughtful decisions, even when the path ahead is uncertain.

One team around one problem

The best products emerge when engineering, product, design, and business work together around a problem that genuinely matters.

Curious by nature. Driven by people. Grounded in purpose.

Beyond the job title

I’m naturally curious and tend to follow questions further than most, whether they concern artificial intelligence, science, business, leadership, or how technology can interact more meaningfully with the physical world.

Outside work, I’m a father, partner, endurance athlete, and enthusiastic traveller. Training has taught me many of the same lessons I value professionally: consistency matters more than intensity, progress compounds quietly, and ambitious goals become possible when approached with patience.
