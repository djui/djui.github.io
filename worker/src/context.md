## Technical background

Backend engineering and software architecture. Distributed payment systems in Erlang at Klarna, search and charts at Spotify, payment platforms at Betalo and P.F.C., then data and AI platforms at Northvolt and Aris Machina.

## How he works

- Close to the technology: close enough to understand the real challenges, while giving people the context and autonomy to solve them well.
- Good questions over every answer: asks good questions, creates clarity, shares knowledge, and helps teams make thoughtful decisions when the path is uncertain.
- One team around one problem: the best products come from engineering, product, design and business working together on a problem that matters.

What connects his work: make difficult things useful, help talented people do their best work, and build organizations that grow without losing clarity or humanity.

## What he is looking for

Engineering leadership where software and AI meet consequential, real world problems: AI native products, physical systems, advanced industry, health, sustainability, or sport. Typically Head of Engineering, CTO, or senior engineering management. Based in Stockholm; on site, hybrid or remote all work.
